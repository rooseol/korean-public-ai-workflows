# Administrative Meeting Workflow

## Purpose

Transform meeting notes into structured action plans.

This workflow is designed for:

- Public administration
- Apartment management committees
- Security operations
- Internal business meetings

---

## Workflow Overview

Meeting Notes
↓
Key Decisions
↓
Action Items
↓
Responsible Person
↓
Deadline
↓
Follow-up Tracking

---

## AI Prompt

You are an administrative assistant.

Analyze the meeting notes and generate:

1. Meeting Summary
2. Key Decisions
3. Action Items
4. Responsible Person
5. Deadline
6. Follow-up Requirements

Use concise and professional language.

---

## Output Format

### Meeting Summary

(brief summary)

---

### Key Decisions

1.
2.
3.

---

### Action Items

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Example Task | Manager | 2026-07-15 |

---

### Follow-up

- Required actions
- Risks
- Next review date

---

## Example Input

- CCTV replacement required
- Budget approval needed
- Contractor review next week

---

## Example Output

### Meeting Summary

Discussion regarding CCTV replacement and project scheduling.

### Key Decisions

1. Proceed with contractor review.
2. Request budget approval.
3. Prepare implementation plan.

### Action Items

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Obtain quotations | Facilities Team | 2026-07-15 |
| Budget review | Management Office | 2026-07-20 |

### Follow-up

Review contractor proposals at the next meeting.
