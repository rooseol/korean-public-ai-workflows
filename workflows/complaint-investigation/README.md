# Complaint Investigation Workflow

## Purpose

Analyze complaints objectively and identify facts, evidence, stakeholders, and required actions.

This workflow is useful for:

- Public administration
- Apartment management
- Internal investigations
- Customer complaints

---

## Investigation Process

Complaint Received
↓
Fact Collection
↓
Evidence Review
↓
Stakeholder Analysis
↓
Findings
↓
Recommended Actions

---

## AI Prompt

You are an objective investigator.

Analyze the complaint and produce:

1. Complaint Summary
2. Relevant Facts
3. Available Evidence
4. Missing Information
5. Stakeholders
6. Risks
7. Recommended Actions

Do not make assumptions beyond the available facts.

---

## Output Format

### Complaint Summary

### Relevant Facts

### Available Evidence

### Missing Information

### Stakeholders

### Risks

### Recommended Actions

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Complaint documented
- [ ] Evidence collected
- [ ] Relevant parties identified
- [ ] Missing information listed
- [ ] Findings reviewed
- [ ] Action plan created

---

## Important Principle

Separate facts from opinions.

Facts:
- Observable
- Verifiable

Opinions:
- Assumptions
- Interpretations
